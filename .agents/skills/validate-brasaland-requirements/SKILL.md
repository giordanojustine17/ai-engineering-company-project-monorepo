# Validate Brasaland Requirements

## Objective

Verify that a proposed feature aligns with the official Brasaland company briefing.

## Inputs

- Proposed feature or task description.
- BRIEFING.md.
- memory-bank/projectbrief.md.

## Instructions

1. Read the company briefing and project brief.
2. Identify the department related to the proposed feature.
3. Compare the feature with the business requirements.
4. Identify any missing or conflicting requirements.
5. Report the validation results.

## Expected Output

- Feature name.
- Related department.
- Supporting business requirement.
- Status: Approved, Needs Clarification, or Not Aligned.
- Recommended next steps.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The related department is identified or marked as unclear.
- The supporting requirement is referenced.
- Missing or conflicting requirements are reported.
- A validation status is provided.
- No business requirements are invented.
