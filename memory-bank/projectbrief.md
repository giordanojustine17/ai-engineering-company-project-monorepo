# Project Brief — Brasaland

## Company Overview

Brasaland is a grilled food restaurant chain founded in 2008 in Medellín, Colombia. It operates 14 restaurants across Colombia and Florida, with approximately 115 employees.

## Business Problem

Brasaland relies on spreadsheets, manual processes, and disconnected systems. The company lacks centralized data and real-time visibility across its restaurants.

## Project Objectives

- Modernize Brasaland's digital systems.
- Centralize business and restaurant data.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Support both USD and COP currencies.
- Enable better data-driven decisions.
- Automate repetitive business processes.

## Current Milestone

Establish an AI-driven engineering foundation using a memory bank, agent instructions, reusable rules, and skills.
